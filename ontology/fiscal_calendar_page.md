## Fiscal calendar

Lumora's fiscal year starts on **February 1** and ends on **January 31**.

- **FY Q1:** February 1 to April 30
- **FY Q2:** May 1 to July 31
- **FY Q3:** August 1 to October 31
- **FY Q4:** November 1 to January 31

Do not use calendar quarters (`DATE_TRUNC('quarter', ...)`) for executive or regional reporting.
